Privacy Policy (LGPD / GDPR)

Last updated: 2026-10-28. This static landing page collects no personal data by itself. The Baseware source code is hosted on GitHub and is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.

Data we process

  • Language preference: stored only in your browser's localStorage (pf-lang). It never leaves your device.
  • Hosting logs: GitHub Pages may process standard request logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) to serve and protect the site.
  • External links: GitHub, GitHub Sponsors and other linked services have their own privacy policies.

Cookies and trackers

No cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or third-party trackers are used on this page. There are no accounts, purchases or checkout flows on this site.

Your rights

Under LGPD/GDPR you may request access, correction, portability or deletion of personal data. This landing page stores none on a server; for data held by GitHub, use GitHub's privacy controls. For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/platusforge/baseware/issues.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site or the platform changes. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.
